The FreeBSD Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit dedicated to advancing FreeBSD through both technical and non-technical support.
Funded entirely by donations, the Foundation supports software development, infrastructure, security, and collaboration efforts; organizes events and developer summits; provides educational resources; and represents the FreeBSD Project in legal matters.

==== Advocacy

In the second quarter of 2026, our advocacy work focused on expanding our educational video content, reaching more viewers than ever, bringing the community together for a productive Vendor Summit, and reflecting on the work that is helping sustain and grow interest in FreeBSD.
Here are just a few of the ways the Foundation advocated for FreeBSD in Q2 2026:

* Represented FreeBSD at link:https://osselcna2026.sched.com/event/2JQsf/what-running-freebsd-on-a-modern-laptop-taught-me-deb-goodkin-the-freebsd-foundation?iframe=no&w=&sidebar=yes&bg=no[Open Source Summit North America], with Deb Goodkin, Executive Director of the FreeBSD Foundation, presenting "What Running FreeBSD on a Modern Laptop Taught Me."
* The link:https://wiki.freebsd.org/DevSummit/202606[June 2026 FreeBSD Developer Summit] happened in Ottawa June 17-18, 2026.
