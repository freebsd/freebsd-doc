=== aq(4): Aquantia/Marvell AQtion Ethernet Driver

Links: +
link:https://cgit.freebsd.org/src/tree/sys/dev/aq[aq(4) source] URL: link:https://cgit.freebsd.org/src/tree/sys/dev/aq[]

Contact: Nick Price <nprice@FreeBSD.org>

The man:aq[4] driver supports Aquantia (now Marvell) AQtion multi-gigabit Ethernet controllers, common on add-in cards and desktop and workstation motherboards.

Supported hardware:

* Atlantic 1: AQC100 (SFP+), AQC107, AQC108, AQC109, AQC111 and AQC112, including the S variants.
* Atlantic 2: AQC113, AQC113C, AQC113CA, AQC113CS, AQC114CS, AQC115C and AQC116C.

Link speeds range from 100Mb (10Mb on Atlantic 2) to 10Gb depending on the part; the manual page lists each.
The driver has been tested on AQC107, AQC113C and AQC100 adapters.

Features:

* IPv4 and IPv6 checksum offload and TSO, including over VLANs.
* VLAN tag insertion, stripping and filtering.
* Multiqueue MSI-X with RSS across up to 8 receive queues, using the kernel's RSS key and, with `options RSS`, its indirection table.
* Jumbo frames, software LRO, flow control, and suspend/resume.
* PHY temperature and firmware link counters as sysctls.
* PHY thermal shutdown, armed by default where the PHY has a sensor; after a trip the driver waits for the PHY to cool and restores the link.
* Link changes logged with speed and flow control (plus duplex and EEE on Atlantic 2), and link loss with the PHY fault code and temperature.
* Per-interface debug tracing.

The driver is in CURRENT, where it is part of the amd64 GENERIC kernel, and in stable/15 as the if_aq.ko module.

Work in progress: SFP module diagnostics, TDR cable diagnostics with the distance to a fault, and setters for the RSS key and indirection table.

Thanks to Ed Maste, Adrian Chadd, Kevin Bowling and Alexander Ziaee for their work on and review of the driver.

Help wanted:

* Testing on other boards, especially onboard controllers and Atlantic 2 parts other than the AQC113C.
* Enabling the driver on non-x86 platforms; it currently builds only for amd64 and i386.
