The FreeBSD Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) effort has been continued during Q3 2026.
At last, getting SBOM into FreeBSD source is manifesting itself and hopefully will be finalized in Q4.
Thanks to Pierre Pronchery, there is a Phabricator review ongoing: https://reviews.freebsd.org/D60352[D60352] for FreeBSD source.
Also, ports have one for reviewing: https://reviews.freebsd.org/D60441[D60441] made by Mathieu Arnold.

As an option for SBOM output, there are currently available SPDX 2.2 and Lite 3.0.1.
CycloneDX SBOM tool is in work in progress and still needs some love to be as qualified as the output for SPDX.
In the future, all SBOM outputs will be under one tool, which will be named `sbomtool`, and as both solutions rely on pkgconf, they will have CycloneDX when it lands in FreeBSD source.

Additionally, several new SPDX License Identifiers have been accepted. All three are in FreeBSD source:
* link:https://github.com/spdx/license-list-XML/issues/3007[BSD-2-Clause-pos-unchanged]
* link:https://github.com/spdx/license-list-XML/issues/3000[BSD-Source-Code-no-disclaimer]
* link:https://github.com/spdx/license-list-XML/issues/2939[BSD-Source-alt-GPL]

There are several updates from `BSD-2-Clause` to `BSD-Source-beginning-file` in review:

* link:https://reviews.freebsd.org/D59528[sys/arm/arm/busdma_machdep.c: Correct SPDX-License-Identifier]
* link:https://reviews.freebsd.org/D59527[sys/powerpc/powerpc/busdma_machdep.c: Correct SPDX-License-Identifier]
* link:https://reviews.freebsd.org/D59526[sys/powerpc/powerpc/busdma_bounce.c: Correct SPDX-License-Identifier]
* link:https://reviews.freebsd.org/D59525[sys/x86/x86/busdma_machdep.c: Correct SPDX-License-Identifier]
* link:https://reviews.freebsd.org/D59524[sys/x86/x86/busdma_bounce.c: Correct SPDX-License-Identifier]
* link:https://reviews.freebsd.org/D59523[sys/kern/subr_busdma_bounce.c: Correct SPDX-License-Identifier]

Additionally, many third-party repositories have received SPDX License Identifiers, such as libxo (thanks to Juniper for that), and others.
Some projects do not want headers, and some are still in active development.

If you want to help with this effort:

* Review Phabricator changes.
  SPDX License Identifier corrections are at a very low level to review.
* When pkgconf CycloneDX is available, help review and test it.
